Write a postmortem for $ARGUMENTS.

Timeline from evidence; impact (who, how long, how badly); trigger and underlying causes; what went
well and what didn't in detection and response; actions to prevent the class of problem, each with an
owner and priority. Blameless: focus on systems and safeguards, not people.
