Explain $ARGUMENTS (default: the whole project) to a new developer.

Cover: what it does and for whom; how it's organised (main folders and modules); the main flows from
entry point to result; key data structures; how to run, test and debug it; conventions to follow;
and the places most likely to surprise someone. Keep it to what a newcomer needs in their first week.
